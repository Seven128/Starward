# WeChat Mini Program Shared State And Recovery

This on-demand subdomain node normatively continues shared manipulation, accessibility, Settings, import, notification and recovery invariants across Mini Program Surfaces.

## Cross-Control And State Invariants

- A continuous manipulation, its current value and the primary visual or numerical feedback it changes must remain together in one mobile viewport or one bounded, control-owned scroll surface. A journey must not require scrolling away from the control, manipulating it, and scrolling back to inspect its primary effect.
- When direct co-location cannot satisfy that invariant, use one self-explanatory functional trigger plus the named `source-lift-focus-layer` family or a native Modal with the same ownership. A dropdown inside an active focus layer is an anchored disclosure owned by that layer, not another Modal: it must overlay rather than reflow the owner's scroll geometry, own bounded overflow when necessary, close before its parent, and remain mutually exclusive with sibling disclosures.
- An icon may stand alone visually only when its platform convention and meaning are unambiguous in context; every icon-only control still owns a programmatic accessible name, role and state. Otherwise the trigger pairs the icon with a concise label. Secondary explanation is available through an explicit operable disclosure and is never hover-only.
- Mini Program component/icon implementation follows one Build / Reuse / Buy boundary: reuse the existing semantic-asset component/adapter as the single production extension point, but do not reuse inactive Soft Instruments asset styling as Sky Canvas truth. Generic Search/Filter/Chevron/Clock/Compass/Route/Facility/Cloud/Rain/Wind/Moon symbols may come from one mature lightweight platform/license-compatible icon library projected into local mode-aware assets; Sky Canvas-specific moon-phase/horizon/galaxy/radiant symbols may be bounded project assets through the same adapter. A bounded self-implementation remains allowed when it preserves every applicable `DESIGN.md` token, mode, interaction and accessibility semantic. No option is mandatory merely because it is open source or already installed. A heavy second UI system, an unthemeable dependency, duplicate component behavior, per-screen icon drawing or a second token/icon truth is prohibited without a bounded project-owned exception.
- `observation-mode-control` is rendered only on the standalone Settings child route and explicitly enters or exits the closed warm-red observation presentation without navigating away from Settings. `display-mode-switcher` preserves route, focus order, selection and information hierarchy across day/night/observation; leaving observation returns to the exact prior mode/context. Spot Night consumes the active presentation but does not own a second entry or mode store.
- Settings presents display mode first, then permission/privacy and reminder ownership before data actions, with secondary spot-selection and accessibility preferences remaining reachable below. `nearby-location-preference` writes the existing `ASK_ONCE | MANUAL_ONLY` preference; orientation access and precise contribution coordinates remain per-use controls and must not be rendered as durable global grants. `departure-condition-reminder` and `contribution-status-reminder` are revisioned identity-scoped intent fields in the same `UserPreferences` owner; UI acknowledgement says only that intent was saved and never claims WeChat subscription delivery without a platform receipt. `account-data-export` consumes a server-generated identity-scoped versioned snapshot and exposes a real platform file/share result;
  it cannot compose the authoritative export from local cache. `account-delete` requires explicit destructive confirmation, an idempotent server receipt and session-invalidating readback before local reset; its copy discloses retained de-identified moderation/audit integrity evidence. Local cache clearing is a separate non-account action and must not fabricate export/deletion completion.
- `profile-link-open-copy` always retains Copy when Try Open is gated, denied or fails. URL save and open reject dangerous schemes and invalid/unsupported destinations without losing the draft.
- Import controls preserve edited fields across parser failure/retry. `import-spot-association` treats `spot_id` and `spot_proposal_id` as disjoint states; submission of a proposal never creates a formal point before its independent review.
- `notification-feedback` is the sole shared Mini Program presentation family for user-visible permission, stale, offline, provider/recovery and acknowledgement feedback. Its semantic tone (`info`, `warning`, `error`, `success`) is independent from its placement. The `inline` placement participates in the local owner's normal flow and carries persistent/actionable states without auto-dismissing the only recovery route. The `floating` placement is reserved for non-critical acknowledgement/light feedback, overlays inside the active phone safe area, does not alter page/Map/SourceLift/scroll geometry or steal focus, and cannot cover the current primary action.
  Both placements share one compact icon/title/optional-body/at-most-one-trailing-action/optional-dismiss anatomy, target tokens, named 44px actions, non-color-only severity, stable-geometry state-swap motion and reduced-motion substitution. A local owner renders at most one full notification; concurrent states use deterministic `error → warning → info → success` order plus an accessible residual count/queue, and resolving the current item reveals the next without losing its meaning. Map permission/stale/provider, Spot Night offline and My/Plan/Settings stale/provider states must use this family rather than page-local banner markup.
- `page-state-recovery` is the shared recovery family for INITIAL/LOADING/READY/EMPTY/PARTIAL/STALE/ERROR/PERMISSION_DENIED. It never substitutes realistic fake values for missing data and never hides usable static content because a dynamic layer failed.

## Responsive, Motion And Accessibility Contract

- The production surface consumes the 750rpx target with 320/375/430 CSS-pixel checks, safe areas and large-text 2×2 fallbacks. At normal text the Map first-viewport budget keeps one Search row, quick filters, a usable map and bottom navigation visible together; the Finder Sheet is initially `closed`, and `peek` never consumes so much height that the selected spot/map relationship becomes unreadable. Enlarged text may wrap quick filters and summaries but cannot eliminate a usable map or hide the Sheet handle/advanced commit/revert actions. The focused observing-condition controls must reflow/compact at every supported width and large-text mode without internal scrolling, clipped controls or a second visible selected-time value.
  Finder/filter/result, the Map Finder Bottom Sheet and other page owners may retain bounded vertical scrolling for reachability, but every user-phone vertical scroll owner hides scrollbar chrome and consumes zero scrollbar layout width without clipping content or disabling touch/wheel/keyboard scrolling. A visible scrollbar on an external DRA/browser review canvas is outside the phone product surface and does not excuse a scrollbar inside it. Page-level horizontal scrolling is forbidden; only an explicitly owned bounded row or data matrix may scroll horizontally. When such a horizontal indicator is necessary it overlays rather than consumes layout height/width and uses a white semi-transparent treatment legible in the active mode.
- Every actionable control owns at least an 88rpx/44px hit region, has a programmatic accessible name/role/state, communicates selection/risk with more than color, and has a keyboard/accessibility-list equivalent where the native map/canvas is not directly operable. Finder and Observing Conditions compact choices center a 60–64rpx visual capsule inside that stable hit region and use the exact compact typography, lighter periwinkle roles and 36–40rpx optically half-clipped top-right star from `DESIGN.md`; the visible star mass occupies roughly one quarter of the capsule without covering its label, and the star, selected border and label/programmatic state form one non-color selection grammar without changing layout. Selection/deselection motion is interruptible and reduced-motion-safe.
  Observation preserves the geometry but replaces yellow/periwinkle with closed warm-red roles. Pointer hover never creates an extra outline or enlarges geometry; keyboard focus remains visible and hugs the visible capsule rather than the invisible hit box.
- Notification content height is driven by its actual compact content rather than fixed empty space. At 320/375/430 and large text, title/body/action reflow without clipping, oversized controls or horizontal overflow; a floating notification retains invariant owner geometry, and a single inline notification must preserve a useful primary surface rather than replacing Map/content with blank space. Day/night/observation use semantic-role parity, with observation restricted to its closed black/warm-red roles and no white transition frame.
- Press, sheet, state and mode transitions follow the DESIGN target; gestures are interruptible and retarget from live position. Reduced motion replaces spatial travel with immediate or at-most-100ms opacity changes while preserving state feedback.
- Day/night/observation retain semantic role parity. Observation is a closed black/warm-red palette; media is opt-in, skeletons cannot flash white, and system/native boundaries must avoid an unowned bright transition.
