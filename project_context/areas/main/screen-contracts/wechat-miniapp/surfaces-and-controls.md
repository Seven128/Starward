The compact sticky `基本信息/地形/天文` text navigation follows the sole section-navigation contract in [Spot and sky](spot-and-sky.md); the lower action region retains `想去/云观星/分享`. ### `miniapp-my-library`

- Primary question: What is my account state, and how do I reach my plan or settings without turning the root into a second content browser?
- Account export and deletion require a fresh identity check bound to the initiating account, in addition to its authenticated session; confirmation dialogs alone are not reauthentication. The client supplies a fresh native WeChat login code, the API verifies the same identity before either operation, and failures retain the existing session/data. Local fixture identity checks are not real WeChat verification. Transport entrypoints: `services/api-client.ts`, API `auth-service.ts` and `controller.ts`; codes stay transient and must not enter logs or durable storage.
- Main surface owns: editable avatar/nickname, one Settings gear, near-term 观星计划 card and 观星点创建与反馈 records under [My account and plans](map-and-finder.md#my-account-and-plans), plus recoverable child routes. Settings owns the single day | night | observation mode control.
- It forbids: peer My/Favorites/Plan/Settings tabs; the subtitle “收藏、计划与显示偏好”; any Favorite count, row, list or duplicate browsing route on My; separate day/night tabs plus an observation CTA, a binary Switch pretending to own three modes, copied commerce/membership/promotion modules, remote/second-family icons, fake statistics, banner walls, page horizontal scrolling, sample content cards, duplicate spot information, and dynamic-provider failure deleting the static favorite relation retained by Search/Map.

### `miniapp-contribution-intake`

- Primary question: How can an authenticated user submit a field report, correction or proposed place without publishing an unreviewed fact or leaking private location/media metadata?
- Main surface owns: an existing formal-spot context when supplied by the information panel; a full prefilled editable site document and immutable baseline/version; field/media differences and a frozen pending receipt; explicit rights and precise-location consent; bounded media selection/upload; multiple manual remote new-place drafts (feedback has no drafts) and upload state; idempotent submit/retry; and the current user's pending/approved/rejected history. Its presentation is one keyboard-safe scroll owner with quiet Back/title chrome, compact divider-backed rows for simple fields, top-label groups for multiline/topic/media controls, conditional new-place location, inline validation, upload progress/recovery and exactly one final submit. Map/new-place and panel/existing-spot entries converge on the same form owner. My opens creation and feedback records including private drafts; rejected records can be edited and resubmitted through the same owner, under the Map/My record contract.
- It forbids: automatically confirming a submitted claim, automatically collecting the contributor’s current device location for existing-spot feedback, retaining raw EXIF, accepting unsupported MIME or oversized files, submitting without explicit rights for media, disclosing another contributor's data, creating a formal spot or changing published facts before owner review plus canonical completeness reassessment, a separate wizard state invented only for layout, per-field cards, reserved empty helper gaps, duplicate sticky/document submits, or controls hidden behind the keyboard.

## Shared liquid-glass material

<!-- ty-context-controlling-source domain="design" path="docs/design-resources/wechat-miniapp/shared/liquid-glass/README.md" -->

My plan card and celestial detail modal share one material responsibility: transmission of the actual backdrop, edge refraction/reflection, bounded blur, theme and accessibility/performance fallback. The consumers own content, typography, geometry, focus, gestures and lifecycle of their screen; a reusable material must not become a shared modal/card business controller. Light/dark/red are parameters of one implementation, not copied page CSS. See the [candidate resource and research](../../../../../docs/design-resources/wechat-miniapp/shared/liquid-glass/README.md); this is the consolidated design reference for the shared material, not a completed production renderer.

Keep recognizable underlying colors/details instead of covering them with an opaque tint or large blur. In sky, preserve the real catalog canvas behind the surface, suppress duplicate background labels while detail is open, and never add decorative astronomical imagery to make glass visible. Reduced transparency and unsupported rendering use an explicit readable fallback. Verify changing backdrops and edge bending with a separate diagnostic surface as well as actual pages; a visually similar border alone does not establish refraction.

The desired visual direction includes smooth curved-edge lensing, directional thin reflections and enough surface separation on light and dark backgrounds, with undistorted foreground text. Transparency alone is insufficient. User review selects the design direction; the [shared rendering boundary](../../../../architecture/runtime-and-domain.md#shared-material-rendering-boundary) owns the unselected production route and the bounded pre-integration device comparison. Do not require completing the whole feature before resolving material feasibility, or treat a design approval as device acceptance.

The current material includes moderate backdrop blur: underlying color and broad shapes transmit while fine details soften; foreground text remains sharp. “High transmission” does not require a nearly unblurred center. Both consumers use the same material parameter owner.
