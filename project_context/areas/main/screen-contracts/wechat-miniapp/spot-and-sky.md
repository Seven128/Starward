# WeChat Mini Program Spot And Sky Invariants

This on-demand subdomain node normatively continues `Cross-Control And State Invariants` for Spot Detail, Spot Night, Sky and the source-lift focus family.

## Cross-Control And State Invariants

- `spot-segment-tabs` contains Overview/Guides/Site and preserves the formal spot identity while each section owns loading/error/retry. Selection moves one stable-geometry active indicator and replaces the content with the target 160ms opacity/state-swap grammar; reduced motion uses an immediate or at-most-100ms opacity equivalent. The prior section is hidden from accessibility and interaction as soon as the new section commits, and rapid retargeting cannot expose overlapping interactive sections. `spot-night-entry` is one flat whole-row route immediately after `spot-tonight-decision` and before `spot-segment-tabs`, remains present regardless of the active Detail section, and pushes the astronomy child `spot/sky` with the same `spot_id`, date/time, timezone and data/algorithm revision.
  Its Tier-A horizon/constellation icon, `今晚夜空` label, secondary summary derived from that same committed local time and trailing chevron form one accessible action; it is not a large full-width primary button and does not reuse the Favorite star. Back returns to the same detail section/scroll position. A date/time change refreshes Tonight/Sky dynamic data without re-requesting immutable facilities/media unless their freshness policy requires it.
- `spot/sky` is the astronomy-information parent for `sky/detail`. `sky-orientation-entry` pushes `sky/detail` only after binding the current formal `spot_id` and the single committed Observation Context. The child shows the inherited place and local time without creating another store. Back restores the astronomy page's exact committed time, useful scroll position and logical opener focus; returning never resets to “now”, remounts a different spot or loses the selected target/mode context.
- `spot-tonight-decision` consumes the server-owned `TripDecision`, which references the same server-owned `SkyOpportunity` used by the Spot Night timeline. `SkyOpportunity` owns per-slice sky evidence and the primary/backup continuous windows; `TripDecision` accepts only a formal spot and applies access, openness, road, legal and safety blockers before any suitability judgment. Any applicable trip blocker produces an effective “不建议” conclusion; a high sky score, explanatory score or user preference cannot override it. Neither Detail nor Spot Night may recreate these rules or substitute an all-night average for the primary window.
- `spot-night-weather-evidence` consumes the BFF's single normalized weather composition. The ordinary conclusion uses the declared primary timeline and labels an Open-Meteo substitution as a visible primary fallback; it never merges models by choosing the clearest result. The professional matrix exposes total/low/mid/high cloud, applicable model-consistency label and provider validity without converting missing fields to zero. Active material official alerts are visible hard blockers. If the official alert feed is unavailable or expired, sky-only evidence may remain readable but the formal-spot trip conclusion cannot be positive and must explain that the safety input is unavailable. Provider/model names, run/valid time, limitations and all contributors to a fused value remain reachable through the existing SourceLift owner rather than through debug IDs or product-version copy.
- `spot-header-actions` keeps page-level actions out of content cards. `spot-favorite-action` is the accessible star state beside the spot name, uses no visible enclosing button border while preserving the full 44px hit region, and exposes pressed/selected/busy semantics independent of its animation. Inactive night mode is an unfilled white outline; activation optimistically fills pale yellow while the dominant main star rotates/scales and at most three smaller differently sized satellites enter from outside its non-interactive effect stage, settle and stop. Their short tapered tails remain faint and static while active; combined satellites stay visually subordinate to the main star. The bounded ritual runs once, does not loop or glow, retargets from its live state under rapid taps and queues nothing.
  Deactivation restores the outline and fades tails/satellites; reduced motion removes travel/rotation and preserves a short fill/opacity state change. Observation uses the same geometry and only warm-red roles. A failed commit retargets visibly to the authoritative relation and emits `notification-feedback`; Finder `想去` and Detail must remain consistent. `spot-navigation-action` is one quiet trailing `去这里 →` text action in the identity/header distance-route row, performs the same safety/blocker check before external handoff and never repeats inside `spot-facility-evidence`. `spot-night-entry` appears once in the upper Detail hierarchy at its fixed decision-to-tabs position and is not duplicated as a generic content-card action. The identity header does not carry generic `地点资料可用`/`今晚条件会变化` chips;
  their meaningful evidence belongs to Tonight, source, freshness and facility content. Existing DESIGN spacing tokens separate Detail cards; facilities content owns facts, not page-level action chrome. Finder `想去` and `spot-favorite-action` retain the same underlying favorite relation when dynamic summaries are stale or unavailable.
- `sky-time-scrubber`, `sky-map-canvas`, `sky-professional-matrix`, `sky-target-list` and `sky-orientation-entry` share one versioned spot/time context. Every rendered astronomy value is attributable to that context and exposes reachable source, validity/update time and partial/stale/unavailable meaning; missing facts are never replaced with prototype values. Tier-A semantic icons improve scanning for twilight, cloud, moon, rain, wind, sources and targets, while the aligned track/matrix remains the value owner and no metric becomes an isolated decorative card. Sensor changes presentation orientation only; it does not alter astronomical truth or the committed time.
- `sky-orientation-sensor` is sensor-follow-only. After explicit permission it consumes the platform orientation stream, exposes calibration/accuracy state and updates the canvas direction continuously; it has no manual toggle, stepper, direction drag or separately editable heading. Before permission, `sky-orientation-recovery` uses one concise, rounded, stable-geometry panel with a compass/orientation icon, one friendly title, one short privacy/usage sentence, one compact primary action and a quiet defer route; it does not repeat policy prose, display an oversized full-width administrative CTA or expose a simulation warning as normal product content. Denied, calibrating and unavailable reuse that geometry and change the minimum necessary copy/action.
  When the stream is denied, unavailable, stale or below the accepted quality boundary, the canvas stops claiming a current heading and the truthful state plus `sky-orientation-object-list` remains operable; the client never synthesizes motion or silently falls back to manual input. The top status is one short named state rather than a two-line badge.
- `source-lift-focus-layer` remains the reusable interaction family only where a compact source control must temporarily reveal a dense locally complete task without changing page scroll geometry. Its current Mini Program use is `mapCoupled` for `观测条件`; Finder no longer uses `panelOnly`. The family states remain idle → lifting → focused → restoring/cancelled and own source anchor, geometry-preserving placeholder, safe-area destination, scrim, explicit close route, Back/Escape, focus return, interruptible reversal and reduced-motion substitution. `mapCoupled` rapidly fades the lifted `观测条件` source into its compact information panel while translating/resizing—but never fading, cloning or remounting—the same physical map into a companion dock; this upper panel never becomes a scroll container. Finder instead stays parallel to the live map through its own Bottom Sheet extent state.
- This named Map analysis focus pattern supersedes the prior permanently adjacent `map-analysis-time-panel` composition and its claim that Map relocation was inactive. Precise date/time selection may still use a smaller route-owned picker sharing `map-time-control`; the existing formal Spot Night time-focus behavior remains owned by `spot/sky` and is not changed by this Map contract.
