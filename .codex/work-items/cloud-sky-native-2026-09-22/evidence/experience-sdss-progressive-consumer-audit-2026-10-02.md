## Present behavior versus candidate risk

The accepted SDSS v1 path requests **JPEG**, and the actual [hook](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/use-sky-sdss-optical.ts) lines 24–59 returns the requested ready field plus its closest ready wider field, from the same publication. [Scene](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/sky-scene-render.ts) lines 214–242 submits wider then finer as `optical-cutout`, opacity 1. [GPU](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/sky-gpu-renderer.ts) lines 125–163 and 969–993 uses straight-alpha source-over plus an 8%-of-field display edge taper. Opaque fine interior therefore replaces coarse RGB; outside the finer field the coarse remains, and the taper mixes the two. This is **not additive double counting in the current v1 opaque interior**. Taper and independently supplied v1 JPEG tone/detail consistency are separate quality obligations.

The [actual candidate harness](../scripts/experience-sdss-mosaic-composition-2026-10-02.mts) lines 139–149 overrides this composite for both levels, bypasses the accepted publication/download/decode chain, and uses OVERVIEW as the parent of both finer trial levels. The actual page normally uses the nearest ready wider level. With additive, coarse and fine both add the same mother-image signal in their overlap. With `signal-over`, contribution alpha lets the coarse remain underneath fine: for straight display colors the image part is `Cfine*afine + Ccoarse*acoarse*(1-afine)` rather than one LOD contribution. Thus a valid faint/black fine sample can leave coarse signal even though fine data are available. This is an **unadopted new display-mechanism risk**, already visible in the existing [GPU evidence](../../../../output/playwright/cloud-sky-sdss-mosaic-candidate-1002-r2/result.json), not evidence that old opaque v1 is broken. Reusing one mother RGB is necessary for tone consistency but does not itself prevent repeated composition.

## Actual W3 owner and wide layer

Selected W3 has only **one** `canvasDeepSkyImage`: [page](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/spot-sky-page.tsx) lines 1548–1591, 1720–1816. Once the preferred decode succeeds it replaces that single bitmap; it is not submitted together with an independent selected parent. [Scene](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/sky-scene-render.ts) lines 196–210 therefore makes one `infrared-cutout` call, with opacity .58. Its shader multiplies encoded source alpha by a brightness display curve and the edge taper, then uses additive RGB. W3 finite PNG holes, display darkness and field edge are separate factors in this calculation, even though their final product is one alpha.

The optional [wide W3 hook](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/use-sky-wide-field-w3.ts) lines 19–22, 35–57 admits only FOV >=60 degrees, sun <=−12 degrees, exact observer frame and selected order-0 tiles; it has one published resolution and returns only wanted tiles. Selected W3 [zoom](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/sky-zoom.ts) lines 50–55 admits FOV <=15 degrees. When desired selected level becomes null, the page clears both selected asset and decoded bitmap; same-object refinement retention applies while a desired level exists. Hence **stable page eligibility domains are disjoint**. A direct scene caller can pass both wide and selected images, and then wide source-over (.48 times twilight strength) precedes selected infrared additive; this API possibility is not proof that the current page produces a sustained overlap. A previous informal “<=12° / retained wide overlap” inference was corrected by these actual owners. Live camera/React transition timing was not exercised, so even transient page overlap remains unverified.

Wide exit on toggle, FOV <60, sunlight, missing time/frame or OBSERVATION returns an empty tile list and deactivates its shared image owner; decoded out-of-view faces are suspended, not reused as coarse fallback. Selected exit on no desired level, changed object or OBSERVATION clears its selected bitmap; old native handles cannot cross canvas generation. Scene also suppresses selected cutouts and all HiPS drawing in OBSERVATION. No current-source evidence shows a persistent wrong wide/selected W3 composition after these conditions settle. This old two-purpose boundary must not be relabeled as M51 candidate coarse/fine duplication.

The galaxy backdrop is another responsibility: scene lines 162–184 passes a null 2MASS image when wide tiles exist and can draw the schematic Galactic band below W3. It does not thereby submit a second W3 LOD. Broad selected W3 can also sit above the schematic/2MASS backdrop when that independent backdrop is eligible. Such layer composition does not claim a single physical spectrum measurement; the next target-optical LOD owner must not silently merge these independent spectra.

## Data coverage, display alpha and source credit

[Accepted SDSS contract](../../../../packages/miniapp-contracts/src/sdss-optical-publication.ts) lines 34–63 contains fixed registry identities/scales, 512-pixel assets and provenance; [hook](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/use-sky-sdss-optical.ts) line 36 hardcodes JPEG. [Scene field arguments](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/sky-scene-render.ts) lines 34–37 carry image, level and fieldDegrees only. They have **no independent per-pixel fine sample coverage, display contribution meaning, format, or general CRPIX**. Current registry scale/JPEG geometry cannot directly admit the new mosaic's three PNG families/geometry by changing only a file extension or hash.

W3 [publication version](../../../../packages/miniapp-contracts/src/deep-sky-image-publication.ts), [server](../../../../workers/miniapp-api/src/deep-sky-imagery.ts) lines 220–251 and [request](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/deep-sky-image-request.ts) lines 75–117 preserve versioned publication identity, source ID, pixel size, aggregate nonfinite count and optional byte-bound display support. Current finite PNG alpha is produced from nonfinite nearest HiPS samples by [source owner](../../../../data-pipelines/deep-sky/allwise_finite_tan.py) lines 61–75, 147–179; finite black/negative values are not missing. These PNG source masks are not the new optical contribution alpha. The W3 transport specifically admits 8-bit RGBA PNG (IHDR color type 6), so the trial's opaque lossless RGB PNG is not directly compatible with this old request/response contract either. The generic native-image request can decode PNG, but selecting a format is not admission of changed semantics or coverage.

[Display support](../../../../packages/miniapp-contracts/src/sky-image-display-support.ts) explicitly describes encoded nonzero RGB, including colors at zero alpha because straight-alpha filtering may mix neighbors. It supports viewport display eligibility/cue retirement, **not source validity or the rule for replacing coarse samples**. An aggregate missing count likewise cannot locate fine holes. The artwork shader has only `u_image`, alpha/display opacity and display taper controls; no independent coverage sampler or finer-footprint exclusion exists. For a new contribution image, `source.a` alone cannot simultaneously mean display opacity and “fine can replace this coarse sample.” Ready/loading image status is also not per-pixel coverage.

Current source attribution follows a completed draw: scene records only successful actual submissions; failed fine keeps coarse credit, failure of both optical fields permits independent W3 fallback, and landscape occlusion can retain visible wider credit when the fine field is covered. Page lines 1404–1435 maps returned image identity back to the actual fine/coarse field and publication. Selected W3 display-support culling and landscape checks avoid known empty/covered viewport credit. Page lines 2474–2480 and 2584–2597 gates presented W3 on current mode/context/time and native canvas, and optical credit follows the last committed canvas while loading, with current-publication checks for update warnings. These are existing owner semantics, not evidence that a task override with a fully transparent/zero-effect optical image has visible provenance: the task's suppressed-success counterfactual was already excluded from actual source credit in the independent GPU review. A new transparent optical family needs its declared display support/composition outcome carried to source/picking consumers without converting darkness into missing science.

## Recovery to preserve and repair boundaries

- **Transport/decode failure:** SDSS hook selects the closest ready wider field; a ready finer field can retain its partial footprint while zooming out until a wider replacement arrives. Shared loader [lines 69–144](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/sky-artwork-loader.ts) has two concurrent loads, byte-budget trimming, no automatic repeated retry of error entries, cancellation/request identity fences and disposal. Native-image [hook](../../../../apps/wechat-miniapp/src/features/sky/use-sky-artwork.ts) lines 21–60 fences canvas/revision/publication and clears the state graph on retirement. A late ready bitmap is released. Preserve this owner rather than inventing another downloader/cache per target or frame.
- **GPU failure:** scene keeps any successful coarser optical field; if neither optical field draws, it uses the independent W3 fallback. Shared loader retires the actual failed bitmap, and explicit retry reports whether GPU reset is needed. Page lines 1956–1970 resets native canvas only for the shared loader's GPU failure, so an ordinary failed HTTP refinement keeps valid coarse pixels. The older selected-W3 retry at lines 1980–1987 always resets its GPU owner and retains the recovery file for a fresh canvas decode; it is a separate existing path, not the new M51 LOD defect.
- **Offline master/transfer:** `sdss_gri_tan.py` owns actual coherent sample availability, same-field weights and mother RGB/pyramid. Keep scientific availability independent of the display transfer; partial finite inputs remain partial and true zero/negative remain data. Do not second-subtract sky or invent morphology. Source full coverage is not quality certification.
- **Versioned publication/transport:** the shared miniapp contracts and server publication owner must bind adopted format, WCS/CRPIX, level footprint, display semantics and coverage identities while keeping v1 immutable consumers compatible. Do not repurpose W3 `sourceFiniteMask`, optional display support or task-only overrides to admit new optical data.
- **Shared scene/GPU composition:** `SkySdssOpticalField` / `drawSkyScene` and `SkyRenderSurface.artwork` / artwork shader are the actual repair entrypoints. Establish one field-group rule for choosing/combining coarse and fine **before** applying its display blend against the real background. A valid fine sample, even if display black/transparent, replaces coarse by independent availability; a missing fine sample keeps coarse, exterior remains coarse and bounded level transition weights must sum to one. For example a two-sampler field-group shader can select fine inside valid fine support and otherwise coarse, or interpolate geometric LOD weights once, then blend the resulting one display contribution once. This is an option for the owner, not an adopted design. Simply changing blend mode, fading coarse by display alpha, removing coarse globally, or using encoded-RGB support as validity does not satisfy these cases.
- **Actual consumers/acceptance:** return successful actual source identities/visible contribution and preserve picking, landscape masking, failure, stale publication and generation behavior. Use the existing whole-mother/three-level outputs and already captured dark/day/twilight scenes for fair follow-up, with a meaningful partial/black fine case at the owner. No broad publication before the new display/geometry/coverage contract and target-runtime path are verified.

Native canvas transitions, phone quality, real resource peaks, server cost/capacity and whole-experience acceptance remain unverified. This audit closes the read-only owner question and leaves adoption with the unique Goal/PLAN owners.
