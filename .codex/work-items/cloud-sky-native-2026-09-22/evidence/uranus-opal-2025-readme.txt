OPAL Uranus Cycle 33 Maps README file

Uranus data were acquired with WFC3/UVIS between October 23, 2025 07:18 UT and October 24, 2025 18:29 UT

Images were navigated using an ellipsoid limb fitting technique with equatorial and polar radii of 25559 and 24973 km, respectively.  The FQ727N data were fringe corrected post-pipeline, resulting in an ~1.1% correction. Each map was generated at the sub-earth longitude +/-90 at 2 pixels/deg resolution, between -90 and +90 deg planetographic latitude; this oversamples the data.  Maps were mosaicked to cover 360 deg of longitude (right edge = 0 deg E. longitude, increasing to the left) and seams between maps were interpolated to smooth, where needed.  The table below lists the Minnaert correction (ratio of cosines of incidence and emission angles to the k power) applied to remove limb darkening for each map.  The mosaics are available in TIF (arbitrarily scaled) and FITS.  The FITS values can be converted to I/F by multiplying by the values in the table below.  Two global maps exist for each filter, covering two full rotations of Uranus. Color maps (TIFF only), are generated from the F657N (red), F547M (green), and F467M (blue) filters, with slight contrast enhancement.  In addition, northpolar maps were generated in the F845M and FQ619N filters.  In these maps, 0 deg longitude is to the right.
