Decode completion only detaches native decode callbacks. Lease retirement releases the file owner, cancels pending decode eligibility, clears native-image eligibility, and notifies the loader. The loader synchronously removes ready/retained maps and cold files and latches failure; pose changes do not initiate replacement downloads. Explicit retry creates a fresh acquisition/Canvas decode. Session-only fixture requests keep their existing file ownership and compatibility behavior.
