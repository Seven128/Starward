OPAL Saturn Maps README file

Cycle 32 Saturn data were acquired with WFC3/UVIS between August 29, 2025 05:53 to August 29, 2025 23:47 UT.

Images were navigated using an ellipsoid limb fitting technique with equatorial and polar radii of 60268 and 54364 km, respectively.  The FQ727N and FQ889N data were fringe corrected post-pipeline, resulting in an ~2% correction. Each map was generated at the sub-earth longitude +/- 90 at 5 pixels/deg resolution, between -90 and +90 deg planetographic latitude .  Maps were mosaicked to cover 360 deg of longitude (left edge = 360 System III W. longitude, decreasing to the right) and seams between maps were interpolated to smooth, where needed.  The table below lists the Minnaert correction (ratio of cosines of incidence and emission angles to the k power) applied to remove limb darkening for each map.  The mosaics are available in TIF (arbitrarily scaled) and FITS.  The FITS values can be converted to I/F by multiplying by the values in the table below.  Two global maps exist for most filters, covering two full rotations of Saturn. Color maps (TIFF only), are generated from the F631N (red), F502N (green), and F395N (blue) filters, with slight contrast enhancement.  ARTIFACTS: Multiple moons (and their shadows) can be seen crossing the mosaics.
