The image is a historical 750 nm grayscale mosaic with WMS resampling and
JPEG artifacts. It is not natural color, current lunar imagery or calibrated
visual photometry.

Original missing-data regions also occur at middle and low latitudes. Some
appear as dark rectangular patches; these are not terrain or current shadows.
This opaque JPEG has no validity mask. Do not infer a missing-data mask by
thresholding near-black JPEG pixels: compression and real low-albedo terrain
must be distinguished from the original product's explicit NoData values.
